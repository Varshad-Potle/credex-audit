## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes. No credit card, no account, no trial period. The audit is free because it is a lead-generation tool for Credex. We make money if you buy discounted AI credits through us — and only if that saves you money.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
Every number in the audit engine traces to an official vendor pricing page, verified during submission week. Sources are listed in our public pricing data file. We update pricing weekly.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
Your audit results are stored so your shareable URL works. If you submit your email, that is stored in our database. We do not sell your data. Identifying details are stripped from the public shareable version of your audit.

**Q: What AI tools do you support?**
Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add tools based on demand — if yours is missing, email us.

**Q: What is Credex?**
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted. The discount is real. SpendAudit is a free tool we built to help teams find where they are overspending. For teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex credits are often the solution.s
